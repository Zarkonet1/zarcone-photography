Most of what I shoot happens on a field, a court, or a mat, with a schedule set by somebody else's season. This Saturday is different. I'll be standing still for a change, at a booth, at the inaugural **Wagner Farm Harvest Festival & Artisan Market** — and I wanted to explain why, beyond "it sounded fun."

## The Short Version

**Wagner Farm Arboretum · Saturday, September 26 · 10 AM–4 PM · Warren, NJ · Free admission, rain or shine.**

Zarcone Photography is a confirmed artisan vendor, set up against the Arboretum's red silos alongside an artisan market, food trucks, live music, and family activities for the day. [Event details →](https://www.wfafnj.org/wfaharvestfestival)

## Why a Farm Festival, Not Just a Field

A lot of what I post lives on team hub pages and in client galleries — real work, but work most people only see if they already know to look for it. A festival booth flips that. It's a few hundred families who weren't necessarily searching for a sports or senior photographer, walking past a table of actual prints, in actual light, on an actual fall afternoon. That's a different kind of introduction than a website ever gives, and honestly, it's good for me too — most of my year is spent editing alone at a desk between shoots.

It also lines up with where the business already lives: Bridgewater-Raritan, Somerset County, the towns and school communities I already shoot for. Wagner Farm is a few miles from most of that. Showing up in person, at a community event, in the same county where the BRHS teams and senior classes I photograph actually live, is worth more than another ad impression.

## What's at the Booth

Stop by and you'll find prints across everything the business covers — sports and game-day action, senior portraits, family sessions, and the school media partnerships (BRHS Football, Volleyball, and Wrestling) I've built out over the past year.

There's also a giveaway running for the day: **enter to win a Zarcone Photography portrait session.** No purchase necessary, one entry per person, and the winner gets a direct follow-up after the festival wraps. I built a quick-scan landing page for it — [zarconephotography.com/harvestfest](/harvestfest) — so instead of filling out a form at a table with a pen that may or may not work, you can scan the QR code at the booth, browse a few images from whatever you're actually interested in — [sports](/sports), [seniors](/senior-portrait-photographer-nj), [family](/portraits), [schools](/schools-athletic-programs-nj), or [events](/events) — and enter right from your phone.

## I'll Also Be Shooting It

Between conversations at the booth, I'll be walking the festival with a camera. It's the Arboretum's first year running this event, which means there's no library of past-year photos to point to — so if it goes well, this year's images become that library. Worth being there for, camera in hand, either way.

If you're in the area Saturday, come find the booth, say hello, and enter to win. And if you can't make it out but want to see what a Zarcone Photography session actually looks like before you decide, [zarconephotography.com/harvestfest](/harvestfest) is open to browse anytime, festival or not.
