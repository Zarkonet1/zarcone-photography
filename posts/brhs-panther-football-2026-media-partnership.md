Some partnerships happen because someone fills out a form. This one happened because both sides understood what good photography is actually worth to a program.

Zarcone Photography is proud to announce that we are the official media partner of **BRHS Panther Football** for the 2026 season.

This isn't a single game. It's a season.

## What the Partnership Covers

Starting with **Media Day on July 29**, we'll be on-site for full team and individual athlete portraits — the kind that end up on banners, in programs, on walls in the weight room, and in recruiting profiles. Clean, professional, true to who this team is before they've played a single snap.

Zarcone Photography is also a **Gold Level Sponsor** of Panther Football for the 2026 season — a partnership we're proud to carry into the stands, onto the broadcast, and throughout the community.

From there, coverage runs through the season. Regular season home games are covered in full — the plays, the sideline moments, and the emotion that live broadcast cameras don't slow down for, all appearing on Big State Sports. Senior Night gets the dedicated treatment it deserves, including a custom commemorative poster design for every graduating senior. And when the postseason arrives, we'll be there for that too — because the season doesn't end when the regular schedule does.

Every image is professionally edited and delivered to a private gallery for download, sharing, and print ordering.

## Why This Matters Beyond the Highlight Reel

A program that invests in its own documentation is a program that takes itself seriously. Coaches know that recruiting starts earlier than it ever has, and the images a player sends a college program — or that a program uses to represent itself — carry real weight.

Game day photography done right isn't just a nice-to-have. It's part of how a program communicates its identity: to recruits, to the community, to the families in the stands who are watching their athlete compete for the last time as a Panther.

That's what this partnership is built around.

## For BRHS Families

Your athlete is going to have a season. There will be moments — a block, a catch, a celebration in the end zone, a sideline conversation with a coach that means something — that don't replay.

Our job is to be in the right place to capture them.

Images from game coverage will be available through the private gallery after each event. Senior Night portraits and poster designs will be coordinated directly with the program. If you want to discuss individual athlete coverage beyond what the partnership includes, [reach out](/about#contact) and we'll make it work.

## For Athletic Directors and Coaches

This partnership continues a long tradition of Zarcone Photography chronicling high school and collegiate sports across New Jersey, New York, and Philadelphia — from wrestling mats to football fields to finish lines. If you run a program in the area and you're thinking about what it would look like to have a dedicated media partner for a season, we'd like to talk.

Not a one-game deal. A season. The kind of coverage that builds a case study, a recruiting library, and a visual record of what your program is.
